RESEARCH 03.A

FLIGHT AS A REPORTED DREAM THEME

In a questionnaire completed by 1,181 first-year Canadian university students, 48.3% reported having experienced a flying dream at least once.

RESEARCH 03.B

FLIGHT AND LUCIDITY

In 1,910 dream reports from 191 participants, people reporting flying dreams were more likely to also report lucid, pre-lucid or false-awakening dreams. This was an association, not proof that flight produces lucidity.

BARRETT · 1991 ↗
RESEARCH 03.C

WHEN ONE PLACE BECOMES ANOTHER

In a small experiment using 20 dream reports containing complete scene and plot discontinuities, judges distinguished intact reports from artificially spliced reports only 57% of the time.

STICKGOLD & SANGODEYI · 2025 ↗
PHYSICAL TRANSLATION

HOW THIS BECOMES A REAL GALLERY

  • Visitors enter through a raised walkway above an LED cloud floor, accompanied by directional air.
  • A projection tunnel makes clouds and stars accelerate around the visitor without requiring a headset.
  • Suspended inverted trees descend into the circulation path using lightweight sculptural forms.
  • Three connected chambers reproduce three sunsets through light, temperature and spatial sound.
  • The final horizon folds through mirrored projection before revealing the light of Room 04.

The room transforms reported dream patterns into an artistic installation. These studies describe specific samples and do not establish universal meanings for flying or falling dreams.
