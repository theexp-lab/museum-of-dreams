RESEARCH NOTE 01.1

ORDINARY LIFE
ENTERS THE DREAM.

WHAT THE STUDY FOUND

In a seven-day study, an average of 83.4% of dream reports were connected to at least one waking-life element.

Among the elements participants could date, 40.2% came from the previous day. Those recent elements were often ordinary rather than exceptional.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE

Dreams do not simply replay daily life and the study does not provide a universal interpretation for particular objects, places or symbols.

INFLUENCE ON THE ROOM

The corridor, clock and repeated doors are familiar everyday elements. The dream reorganises them instead of reproducing a complete real event.

PHYSICAL ROOM CONCEPT 01.2

AN ORDINARY CORRIDOR
THAT REFUSES TO END.

SPACE
A life-sized apartment corridor built with repeating doors, mirrored endings and projection surfaces.
LIGHT
Domestic ceiling lights flicker only after a visitor crosses a doorway. The colour temperature becomes gradually less natural.
SOUND
Hidden speakers repeat the visitor’s footsteps with a slight delay while an impossible clock slowly loses its rhythm.
INTERACTION
Motion sensors change room numbers, exit signs and door destinations whenever the visitor turns away.
MATERIALS
Carpet, painted wood, frosted glass, mirrors, projection screens and concealed speakers.
VISITOR ACTION
The visitor chooses a door and returns to the same corridor, where one familiar detail has changed.
ARTIST PROFILE
Installation artist · scenographer · sound designer · interactive technologist
