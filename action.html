COMPOSITE DREAM 05

There is a motorcycle
waiting under a red light.

You know it is yours.

The engine starts
before you touch it.

The city opens in front of you.

One second later,
the city is moving beneath you.

A red light
moves across the skyline.

Somehow,
you know you have to follow it.

It is getting
further away.

You accelerate.

The signal is waiting
on the next rooftop.

The motorcycle cannot follow.

The distance
is impossible.

You jump anyway.
FOR THREE SECONDS

The entire city

waits below you.

You land
on a moving train.

It was not there a moment ago.

THE BRIDGE ENDS IN

08 SECONDS

There is someone
inside the final carriage.

You came here
to rescue someone.

You still do not know who.

There is no time
to ask.

IMPACT SUSPENDED

For one impossible moment,

nothing is falling
at the same speed.
