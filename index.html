EXPERIENCE 002 · IMMERSIVE EXHIBITION CONCEPT · 2026

WHAT IF THE DREAMS WE FORGET EVERY MORNING BECAME PLACES WE COULD VISIT?

The Museum of Dreams is a temporary immersive exhibition built from dreams submitted anonymously by people around the world.

Artists, filmmakers, sound designers and creative technologists transform selected dreams into physical environments that visitors can enter.

DREAMS ARE PRIVATE.

DREAMS ARE TEMPORARY.

THIS MUSEUM MAKES THEM COLLECTIVE.

01 · THE IDEA

A MUSEUM INSIDE
A SLEEPING MIND

The exhibition building becomes the body of one sleeping stranger. Visitors cross the threshold of sleep, enter an oversized brain and travel through one complete night.

Each room transforms a submitted dream into a different artistic experience using physical sets, sculpture, projection, sound, light, technology and sensory effects.

As the night progresses, the museum becomes darker, stranger and increasingly unstable—until the dream turns into a nightmare and forces the audience to wake up.

02 · VISITOR JOURNEY · 60–90 MINUTES

  1. 01 SUBMIT

    Dreams are collected anonymously from people around the world.

  2. 02 INTERPRET

    Selected creators transform the dreams into multisensory artworks.

  3. 03 ENTER

    Visitors enter the body of a sleeping stranger and cross into the brain.

  4. 04 WANDER

    Every room follows a different dream and artistic language.

  5. 05 WAKE

    A nightmare destabilises the exhibition and chases visitors toward the exit.

  6. 06 REMEMBER

    Visitors leave one of their own dreams for a future edition.

03 · THE PHYSICAL ROOMS

ONE NIGHT.
FIVE ARTISTIC LANGUAGES.

Each category defines an emotional territory rather than a fixed visual formula. The submitted dream determines the final artwork.

ROOM 01

CHILDHOOD

Painted scenery, oversized sculptures, familiar scents and distant childhood recordings.

ROOM 02

ADVENTURE

Playable installations, moving architecture, 3D landscapes, projection and wind.

ROOM 03

ROMANCE

Dance films, translucent fabrics, intimate sound, perfume and faceless human figures.

ROOM 04

ACTION

Responsive lights, powerful bass, practical effects and choreographed physical movement.

ROOM 05

HORROR

The exhibition breaks its own rules. Flashing lights, distorted sound and an invisible presence chase visitors toward the waking world.

04 · SCIENCE AS DRAMATURGY

THE BRAIN NEVER
FULLY GOES QUIET.

Dreams can occur during both REM and non-REM sleep. Memory fragments, emotional processing and sensory associations contribute to their unusual narratives.

The exhibition uses sleep science as a structural language rather than presenting a literal scientific simulation.

The descent toward horror is a creative decision. Longer periods of REM sleep toward morning inspire the increasing visual and emotional intensity.

05 · A LIVING, INTERNATIONAL MUSEUM

THE COLLECTION CHANGES
EVERY TIME THE WORLD SLEEPS.

Dreams are gathered through an online submission room. Contributors can remain anonymous and retain control over how their stories are interpreted.

Every edition invites artists, filmmakers, musicians, sculptors, scenographers and creative technologists from different countries.

The rooms combine painting, sculpture, projections, screens, spatial sound, interactive technology, lighting, scent and carefully designed 4D effects.
