RESEARCH 04.A

DREAMS ARE SOCIAL SPACES

In dream reports collected from 15 students, 83.5% contained at least one social situation. Dreams also contained more social events than waking-life reports from the same participants.

The sample was small and the study provided only partial support for the broader theory being tested.

RESEARCH 04.B

PARTNERS AND FORMER PARTNERS

Across 1,612 dream reports from 425 students, current partners appeared more frequently than former partners.

Dreams involving former partners were often more negatively toned, although they could also contain friendliness and positive emotions. Current-partner dreams were more likely to involve ordinary shared situations.

RESEARCH 04.C

WHAT FOLLOWS INTO THE MORNING

In a two-week diary study, emotions and interactions involving romantic partners in dreams were associated with relationship behaviour the following day.

Dreamed jealousy and infidelity were associated with less intimacy and greater conflict the next day.

These findings describe associations. They do not mean that a dream predicts or determines what will happen in a relationship.

PHYSICAL TRANSLATION

HOW THIS BECOMES A MUSEUM ROOM

  • Two human-scale fields of light respond to visitors by approaching, separating and moving at different rhythms.
  • Amber lighting and controlled radiant warmth make the space physically warmer as the two lights move closer.
  • A third red light enters the installation and disrupts the relationship between them.
  • Spatial audio moves around the room while low-frequency transducers allow the final heartbeat to be felt through the floor.
  • An optional pulse sensor could influence the final light rhythm without recording or storing the visitor’s biometric information.
  • The pulse naturally continues beyond the room and becomes the transition into Action.
CURATORIAL METHOD

The room does not claim to interpret individual dreams. It translates recurring emotional and social patterns into light, temperature, movement and sound.

PROPOSED COMMISSION

You cannot see
who they are. But something feels familiar.

You move closer. The room grows warmer.

For a moment, you seem to be in the same dream.

But the two lights do not move in quite the same way.

Perhaps you are together. Perhaps you are only dreaming of each other.

Whatever happens here may follow you into the morning.

Then a third light appears.

You do not know who it belongs to.

The warmth has changed.

It is not a good feeling.

THAT IS ENOUGH.
